About the Author
K
Kenya Reid-Winecoff
RN, BSN · HealthSim Hub
Kenya Reid-Winecoff, RN, BSN has been teaching Health Science for 12 years — Health Science 1 & 2, Project Lead the Way, and now Medical Assisting. When she started teaching CCMA, she had no curriculum and had to build everything from scratch. The resources she bought weren't what she expected, so she stopped searching for the right resources and started building what actually worked. Games, interactive activities, scenario-based learning — anything to keep students engaged and help the content actually click. Her first semester teaching CCMA, 12 out of 14 students passed the NHA exam. When she realized she needed a more structured way to deliver everything she'd created, HealthSim Hub was born.
